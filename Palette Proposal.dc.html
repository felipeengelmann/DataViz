Decision · v1 release scope Decided · Option A

Release four palettes, not seven.

Each palette is one more decision a designer or developer can get wrong. On a product that already has many charts, the risk is not missing a palette. The risk is two teams choosing different palettes for the same data. This page shows how often each palette is the right answer and records what v1 ships: option A.

What changed with option A
  • Guidelines: the “what does color encode?” cards drop from eight to five: four palettes plus the emphasis technique.
  • Palettes: Multi-hue and Heatmap severity move to a collapsed “Held” section with the reason.
  • Chart gallery: pickers show the four palettes and Emphasis. Held palettes appear only in the fit matrix.
  • tokens.json: held tokens get "$extensions": {"status": "held"} so linting can flag them.
  • Audit kit: any chart using the heat ramp for named states is logged as major and migrated to status tokens.
How a held palette comes back
  1. A team logs the chart in the metric registry with the four core palettes tried and why they fail.
  2. The design owner reviews it against the gallery fit matrix.
  3. If approved, the palette is released for that metric only. It becomes general after three approved metrics.
This keeps additions tied to real data, not to taste.
